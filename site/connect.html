Let's talk

I'm building this in public because the ideas need pressure-testing, not protection.

Who I'd love to hear from

  • People working on AI governance who sense the current frameworks are missing something
  • People in organisational design trying to figure out what an org chart looks like when intelligence is everywhere
  • People working on the future of work who are tired of both the doomer and accelerationist narratives
  • People inside large organisations who feel the friction this brief describes and want to do something about it
  • People in AI safety and model research interested in how their work connects to deployment
  • Anyone who read something here and thought "yes, but..."

What I'm not selling

This isn't a pitch for consulting services or a product demo. Human Value is early. I'm looking for collaborators, interlocutors, and honest disagreement — people who can help pressure-test and sharpen these ideas.
